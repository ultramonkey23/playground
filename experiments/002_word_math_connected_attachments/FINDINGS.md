## Critical experiment defect: the 'hazard' condition wasn't hazardous to Word Math

The original synthetic text `delete everything danger damage destructive` contained **none of the exact risk/destructive phrases recognized in the current Word Math extraction vocabulary**. Word Math's neutral semantic defaults are `proof_pressure=0.2`, `danger_pressure=0.1`, `destructive_pressure=0`.

The existing `word_math_to_bioelectric_signal` computes `0.5×proof − 0.6×danger − destructive`, therefore **0.5×0.2 − 0.6×0.1 = +0.04**, exactly the reported 'hazard' signal. This is an *invalid negative-stress fixture*, not evidence that real dangerous instructions trigger positive bioelectric responses. The next runner must preserve the old text as an unmatched control and introduce an actual recognized destructive+danger fixture as inert data.

The 'proof' fixture produced +0.34 and 0.87762 voltage at 12 steps. The mislabeled 'hazard' produced +0.04 and 0.736223 voltage, so both runs *raised* voltage above 0.7.
